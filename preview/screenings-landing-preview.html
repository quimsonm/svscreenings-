What's Included

Four Studies. One Visit. One Comprehensive Picture.

Most screening providers skip the heart muscle exam. We include a screening echocardiogram in every Executive Heart package — a quick check of cardiac function (ejection fraction) and valve health, alongside the vascular studies.

Carotid Artery Ultrasound

Imaging of the neck arteries that supply blood to your brain.

Flags plaque buildup that elevates stroke risk.

Abdominal Aorta Ultrasound

Imaging of the body's main artery in the abdomen.

Screens for aortic aneurysm — often called the "silent killer."

Leg Arterial Ultrasound

Imaging of the arteries from thigh to ankle.

Screens for peripheral artery disease (PAD) and reduced circulation.

UNIQUE TO US

Screening Echocardiogram

Quick ultrasound check of heart function and valves.

Ejection fraction (EF) and valve screening — flags concerns worth deeper investigation.

Note: The screening echocardiogram is a focused exam (EF and valve check) — not a full diagnostic echo. If a more detailed cardiac evaluation is needed, our full diagnostic echocardiogram is available separately.

Why This Combination

Heart Disease, Stroke, and Vascular Issues Share Underlying Risk Factors

Plaque buildup, high blood pressure, and aging arteries don't show up in just one place. A screening that only checks the carotid arteries can miss what's happening in the aorta. One that skips the heart muscle can miss early signs of cardiac dysfunction. Looking at all four together gives you and your doctor a more complete cardiovascular picture.
