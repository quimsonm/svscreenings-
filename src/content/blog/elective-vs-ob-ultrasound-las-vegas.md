You searched for "OB ultrasound Las Vegas" and ended up here. I get it. And I want to be upfront with you before you read any further.

**We are not your OB's office.** We don't do diagnostic prenatal care. We won't check your cervix or monitor your baby's growth the way your medical provider does.

But here's the thing — that might not actually be what you're looking for.

Because when I talk to the women who call us after finding this page, what they almost always describe isn't a clinical need. It's something simpler.

*They just want to see their baby.*

Not in a fluorescent-lit exam room with a rushed tech and a grainy screen that gets pulled away before you can really look. They want to be present for it. They want their partner in the room. They want to *feel* something instead of just getting a printout handed to them on the way out.

That's a completely different thing. And it has a completely different solution.

**So let me clear this up once and for all.**

---

**Your OB's ultrasound is a medical exam.** It measures. It monitors. It checks fetal position, amniotic fluid, placenta location, and growth percentiles. It's ordered because your doctor needs clinical data to manage your pregnancy. It belongs in your prenatal care.

**Do not skip it.**

**An elective sneak peek ultrasound is something else entirely.** No clinical data. No measurements. No insurance. No referral needed. Just a dedicated session where a trained sonographer focuses on one thing — showing you your baby as clearly as possible. Their face. Their hands. The way they're already curling their little fingers. Their heartbeat, live, on screen.

It's not a replacement for your OB care. It's the moment your OB care doesn't have time to give you.

**And you can have both.**

Most of our patients already have their OB appointments on the calendar. They come to us in between because they don't want to wait eight weeks for the next clinical scan just to get thirty seconds of eye contact with a screen before the appointment's over.

*We give you the whole ten minutes.*
