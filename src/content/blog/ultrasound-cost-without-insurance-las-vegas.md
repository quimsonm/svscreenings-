## What "Cash Pay" Actually Means

Cash pay means you pay a flat price upfront. No insurance company in the middle. No surprise bill three months later.

Here's the part most people don't expect: **a self-pay ultrasound is often cheaper than routing it through insurance.** Insurance billing comes with layers. Coding fees, negotiated rates, administrative overhead. You end up paying for all of it through your deductible.

Cash pay skips the maze. One price. One visit. One answer.

## Real Price Ranges in Las Vegas (So You're Not Guessing)

Every clinic is different, but here's what cash-pay ultrasound pricing generally looks like in the Las Vegas area:

- **Echocardiogram (heart ultrasound):** $350 to $450 depending on the facility
- **Vascular scans** (carotid arteries, aorta, leg arteries, veins): $250 to $325
- **Abdominal ultrasound** (liver, gallbladder, kidneys, pancreas): $250 to $325
- **Pregnancy/OB scans:** varies by gestational age and number of views
