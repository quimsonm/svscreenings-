Critical Production Rules

Use: Google YouTube pre-roll (Las Vegas geo) + Meta retargeting for site visitors who searched for diagnostic ultrasound
Goal: Convert symptomatic patients searching for fast, affordable ultrasound

PROOF

ARDMS-certified sonographers. Board-certified physician interpretation. Written report in two to three business days.
"ARDMS-certified · Physician-read · Report in 2-3 days"

LOCATION

We're in Summerlin, next to Mountain View Hospital. No referral needed. No insurance hassle.
"Summerlin · Next to Mountain View Hospital"

"Book at svscreenings.com · 702.843.6612"

Caption Strategy (Critical for Meta)

Why Hard Captions Matter
  • 85% of Meta videos watched muted — without captions, your ad delivers no message
  • YouTube auto-captions are inconsistent — burning them in guarantees they appear correctly
  • Captions improve retention by 30-50% across all platforms

CapCut Caption Workflow

1Import video — drop your AI avatar clips + B-roll into a CapCut project
2Tap Captions → Auto Captions — CapCut transcribes your audio automatically
3Edit transcribed text — fix any words CapCut misheard, shorten lines if needed (medical terms like "ARDMS" or "echocardiogram" often need correction)
4Style the captions — pick a high-contrast style. Recommended: white text + black background box, or yellow text with thick black outline. Make them readable on any background.
5Position lower-middle of frame — never bottom edge (gets cut off by UI), never top (covers face)
6Animate sparingly — "pop in" entrance is fine. Avoid bouncing/spinning — looks amateur and distracts.
7Export with captions burned in — this makes them part of the video file (not a separate track that can be turned off)

Caption Style Recommendation

EXAMPLE: White Bold Text · Black Outline · Bottom-Center
  • Font: Sans-serif, bold (CapCut's "Bold" or "Pop")
  • Size: Large enough to read on phone — typically 60-80% of safe area
  • Color: White with thick black outline OR white-on-black background box
  • Lines: Max 2 lines at a time, 5-7 words per line
  • Highlight: Consider highlighting key words in gold (matches your brand) — "$250" or "same week" or "this Saturday"

Editing in CapCut — Step by Step

1Set project to 9:16 vertical (1080×1920) — primary aspect ratio
2Import AI avatar clips from your Google Flow exports
3Layer in B-roll cuts on top using picture-in-picture or full-frame cutaways
4Add background music — choose royalty-free, low-energy for Video 2 (medical/calm), slightly upbeat for Video 1 (urgency). Volume: 15-20% so it doesn't compete with dialogue.
5Auto-caption + edit + style per the caption workflow above
6Add brand watermark — small logo in upper-right corner, 60% opacity, visible throughout
7Build end card — solid color background or muted B-roll, large text with URL and phone, animated logo entrance. Hold for 5-7 seconds at the end.
8Review at 1x speed with sound on AND off — confirm message works both ways
9Export at 1080p, 60fps — H.264 codec, MP4 format. Save in CapCut, then export to camera roll.
10Create the 1:1 square version — duplicate project, change canvas to 1080×1080, reposition elements, re-export

Production Checklist Before You Hit Record

AI Avatar Generation (Google Flow)

  • Generate clip for Video 1 hook (4 sec) — urgent but warm
  • Generate clip for Video 1 location segment (6 sec) — confident, neutral
  • Generate clip for Video 2 hook (5 sec) — warm, personal
  • Generate clip for Video 2 solution segment (6 sec) — reassuring
  • Generate clip for Video 2 trust segment (6 sec) — credible, professional
  • Export all clips at highest quality, 9:16 vertical

B-Roll Needed

  • Ultrasound machine close-up (5 sec)
  • Sonographer hands working the probe (5 sec)
  • Monitor showing ultrasound imaging (5 sec)
  • Carotid color doppler on screen (5 sec)
  • Clinic interior wide shot (5 sec)
  • Building exterior shot (3 sec)
  • Physician report on screen / printed (3 sec)

Graphics / Overlays

  • Three-column comparison: ER $3,000 / Insurance 3 weeks / Us $250
  • Four-icon row: Carotid · Aorta · Leg Arteries · Echo
  • Map pin / location overlay with full address
  • End card template (matches landing page brand)
  • Logo watermark file

Compliance Check Before Export

  • No promissory language ("we'll catch your problem" — never)
  • No directives ("you should book today" — soft language only)
  • No diagnostic claims (imaging only — never "diagnosis")
  • Address visible and accurate
  • Phone number readable for 5+ seconds in end card
  • No before/after body imagery (Meta policy)
  • No needles, blood, distressed patients in frame

Post-Launch Iteration Plan

1Run 7 days at $15-25/day per video, monitor CTR and conversion
2If CTR < 1%, the hook isn't working — re-record opening 5 seconds with new hook
3If CTR > 1% but conversion < 1%, the offer or landing page is the issue — not the video
4Make 3 variants per winner — change one element each: hook, music, end card style
5Refresh creative every 14-21 days — Meta ad fatigue is real, performance drops sharply after 3 weeks
