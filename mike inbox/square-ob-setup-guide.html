Square Setup: OB Ultrasound + Dual-Tech Saturdays

Step-by-step guide to add OB services to Square Appointments and configure dual-tech scheduling so Mike and the OB sonographer can run parallel Saturday blocks.

Before You Start

Three things to verify first
  • Square Appointments Plus subscription ($29/month minimum) — free tier doesn't support multiple staff calendars. Upgrade first if needed.
  • Two exam rooms available at Frontline on Saturdays — otherwise dual-tech can't run in parallel.
  • Probes confirmed — curvilinear (3-5 MHz) for transabdominal OB, transvaginal probe (5-9 MHz) for early pregnancy. If missing, order before launch.

Step-by-Step Setup

1

Verify / Upgrade Subscription

Go to Square Dashboard → Account & Settings → Pricing & Subscriptions. Confirm you're on Square Appointments Plus (or higher). If not, upgrade — Plus is $29/month and unlocks multiple staff calendars.
2

Add the OB Tech as a Team Member

Square Dashboard → Team → Team Members → + Add Team Member

  • Enter her full name, email, phone
  • Role: "Sonographer"
  • Permissions: enable Appointments access only — leave financial/admin permissions off
  • Send her login credentials so she can manage her calendar
3

Set Her Working Hours

Square Dashboard → Appointments → Staff → [Her Name] → Hours

  • Saturday: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
  • All other days: Unavailable

This means OB services can only be booked on Saturdays — Square auto-restricts customer-facing availability to her hours.

4

Create the Four OB Services

Square Dashboard → Items & Services → Services → + Add Service

Create each of the four services below as a separate Square service. For each one:

  1. Set the name, duration, price, and deposit
  2. Under "Staff who can perform this service" — only check the OB tech. Uncheck yourself.
  3. Add a clear description so patients know what they're booking

5

Suggested Customer-Facing Descriptions

Limited Pregnancy Ultrasound: "Focused ultrasound to check fetal heartbeat, position, or fluid level. Approximately 30 minutes. Physician-interpreted report in 2–3 business days."

First Trimester / Dating Ultrasound: "Early pregnancy ultrasound to confirm pregnancy, dating, viability, and number of babies. Performed under 14 weeks gestation. Approximately 45 minutes. Physician-interpreted report in 2–3 business days."

Follow-up Pregnancy Ultrasound: "Re-evaluation of a previously identified finding or interval growth check. Approximately 45 minutes. Physician-interpreted report in 2–3 business days."

Anatomy Scan (Pregnancy): "Comprehensive fetal anatomy survey, typically performed at 18–22 weeks. Evaluates fetal structures, growth, placenta, and amniotic fluid. Approximately 60 minutes. Physician-interpreted report in 2–3 business days."

6

Keep Your Existing Services Tagged to You

Go through your existing services (Abdomen, Echo, Thyroid, Renal, Carotid, etc.) and verify they're assigned to you only. This keeps them separate from her OB calendar so both can run in parallel on Saturdays without conflict.

7

Add Buffer Time to Each Service

For each OB service, add 5–10 minutes of buffer in the service settings:

  • Limited: 5 min buffer
  • Dating: 10 min buffer
  • Follow-up: 10 min buffer
  • Anatomy: 10 min buffer

This prevents running late, allows for room turnover, patient prep, and image saving between appointments.

8

Test the Customer-Facing Booking Experience

Open your booking page in an incognito browser window and verify:

  • ✓Selecting "Anatomy Scan" shows ONLY Saturday 9 AM–1 PM slots
  • ✓Selecting "Abdomen Ultrasound" shows your normal Wed/Fri/Sat schedule
  • ✓On Saturday, both you and the OB tech can be booked at the same time slot without conflict
  • ✓Deposit of $75 is charged at booking
  • ✓Service descriptions display correctly

Phased Launch Recommendation

Don't launch all four services at once
  1. Weeks 1–2: Limited + Dating only (simplest exams, lowest liability)
  2. Weeks 3–4: Add Follow-up
  3. Month 2+: Add Anatomy Scan (after tech comfort + radiologist alignment + probe confirmation)

Operational Checklist Before Saturday Launch

  • ☐ Square Appointments Plus subscription confirmed
  • ☐ OB tech added as team member with Saturday-only hours
  • ☐ All 4 (or 2 for phased launch) OB services created with correct staff assignment
  • ☐ Existing services still assigned to you only
  • ☐ Buffer times added to each service
  • ☐ Customer-facing booking flow tested in incognito
  • ☐ Two exam rooms confirmed available at Frontline on Saturdays
  • ☐ Curvilinear + transvaginal probes confirmed (or ordered)
  • ☐ Malpractice insurance OB coverage confirmed with broker
  • ☐ Reading radiologist comfortable with OB anatomy reports
  • ☐ OB-specific report template established
  • ☐ Patient intake updated for OB (last menstrual period, gestational age, etc.)
  • ☐ Image delivery method decided (printouts, USB, secure email)
  • ☐ Spanish-speaking capability planned (Vegas market consideration)

TL;DR Setup Sequence

1. Upgrade Square if needed → 2. Add OB tech → 3. Set her Saturday hours → 4. Create 4 OB services tagged to her only → 5. Add buffers → 6. Test booking flow.

Time required: about 60–90 minutes tonight.
