## Why Traffic (not Leads) for this campaign

The /gift page now exists with Square gift cards built in. The purchase happens on Square — no form needed. A Traffic campaign sends people directly to the page to buy. A Leads campaign would collect a name/phone but adds unnecessary friction when the goal is a same-session purchase.

---

## Performance Benchmark

- **Good:** Cost per link click under $0.75
- **Great:** Cost per link click under $0.40
- **Watch:** If 50+ clicks and zero Square gift card sales — the /gift page or price point needs review

Check after 3 days (May 3). If Version A underperforms, swap to Version B or try a video clip from Wednesday.
