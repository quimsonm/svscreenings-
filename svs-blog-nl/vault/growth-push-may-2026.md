## Major Initiative: `/screenings` Landing Page

**Problem:** Homepage is trust-building, not offer-led. Paid ad traffic for screening keywords lands on a 12-section homepage and bounces.

**Solution:** Dedicated `/screenings` landing page following the same conversion pattern as diagnostic pages.

**Structure:**
- Hero: "$199 Cardiovascular Wellness Screening — Heart, Carotid & Leg Arteries in One Visit" + Book CTA
- What's included (visual breakdown of the 3 studies in Essential)
- Why these 3? (brief education — stroke, aorta, leg circulation)
- Credentials + What Happens After (reuse proven block)
- Upgrade options ($349 Executive most popular, $597 Gold Standard)
- Who should get screened (50+, family history, etc.)
- Social proof + reviews
- Final CTA

**Pricing tier strategy:** Lead with $199 as entry headline. Position $349 Executive as "Most Popular" middle option. $597 Gold Standard as premium anchor.

**Once live:** Repoint existing Smart campaign from homepage → `/screenings`. Measure conversion lift for 2 weeks.

**Expected impact:** 30-50% conversion lift = CAC drops from $98 → $60-70 = same ad budget produces ~30-40% more bookings.
