# Recurring Revenue — Brainstorm & Strategy
**Created:** 2026-04-21
**Status:** Planning phase — prioritized by effort vs. impact

---

## The Gap
Current model is transactional — patient books, gets scanned, leaves. No mechanism to capture annual repeat business, family purchases, or passive referral revenue. Every dollar requires a new acquisition.

---

## All Ideas (Brainstormed)

### 1. Annual Screening Membership — $299–$399/year
Lock in next year's revenue today. Patient pays once, gets annual screening + priority scheduling + 10% off add-ons.

**Pitch after appointment:**
> "Since you'll want to do this again next year, we have an annual membership that locks in today's price and includes priority scheduling. Want me to set that up?"

---

### 2. Family / Couples Plans
One household, multiple scans. Caregiver angle — adult child books for parent.

- Couples Essential: 2 × Essential = $349 (saves $49)
- Family Heart Plan: 2 adults annually = $599/year
- "Bring a Parent" bundle: You + parent = $349

---

### 3. Corporate Wellness Accounts — B2B
Highest revenue ceiling. Local businesses pay annually to screen employees.

- Small business (10–20 employees): $2,000–$4,000/year
- Medium (50+ employees): $10,000+/year

**Best first targets:**
- Construction companies (physical labor, vascular risk)
- Law/accounting firms (sedentary, 45+ partners)
- Casino/hotel management
- Fire departments + police unions
- Real estate brokerages (self-employed, no employer health benefits)

/partners page already exists — needs corporate wellness angle added.

---

### 4. Post-Appointment Email Nurture (Mailchimp)
Every patient enters an automated 12-month sequence:

- Day 3: "Your results are in — here's what to discuss with your doctor"
- 6 months: "A quick check-in — how are you feeling since your screening?"
- 11 months: "Your annual screening is coming up — book now before slots fill"
- 12 months: "It's been a year — time to see what's changed"

Set up once, runs forever.

---

### 5. Physician Referral Network
3–5 primary care physicians referring patients = 2–5 referrals/month each = $500–$1,400/month passive.

**How to approach:**
- Print a one-page referral sheet
- Walk into 5 nearby primary care offices
- Offer a complimentary screening for the physician

---

### 6. Condition Monitoring Package (6-Month)
For high-risk patients — hypertension, family history, post-surgery:

- Semi-annual monitoring: 2 × Essential = $349/year (saves $49)
- Creates twice-yearly touchpoint

**Pitch:**
> "Given your blood pressure history, we'd recommend monitoring every 6 months. We have a monitoring package that covers both visits for $349."

---

### 7. Gift Cards — "Gift of Health"
Seasonal revenue spike. Mother's Day (May 11), Father's Day (June 15), Christmas.

- Gift card: $199–$349
- Positioned as: "The Gift of Peace of Mind"
- Adult children buying for parents = exact target demographic
